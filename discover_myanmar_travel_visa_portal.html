🇹🇭 Thailand Embassy (Yangon)

VFS / Online Booking

Tourist Visa (TR) or E-Visa online appointment system required for submission at Manawhari Road Embassy.

• Documents: Passport (Original + Copy), 6-Month Bank Statement ($1,000+ balance), Flight & Hotel Confirmation, NRC Card.

• Fee: ~$40 USD tourist visa fee.

🇯🇵 Japan Embassy (Yangon)

Direct / JVAC

Short-term Tourist / Business Visa. Requires detailed daily travel schedule in Japan.

• Documents: Visa Form with QR code, Itinerary in Japan, Bank Book with recent transactions, Employment/Company registration letter.

🇸🇬 Singapore Visa

Authorized Agent / E-Visa

Submitted through authorized travel agencies in Yangon or via Singapore local sponsor (V39A form).

• Documents: Passport photo, Form 14A, Letter of Introduction (LOI) from Singapore Citizen/PR if applicable.

🇪🇺 🇰🇷 🇺🇸 Korea, Schengen & USA

VFS / DS-160

US Embassy (Inya Road) requires DS-160 payment and interview. Schengen visas booked via VFS Global Yangon.

• Tip: Ensure ties to Myanmar (property, job, family) are clearly documented for interview success.

SIM Buying Checklist at Airport

  1. Have your original passport ready (airport staff will scan photo page and visa stamp).
  2. Payment is usually accepted in Myanmar Kyat (MMK) cash. Change money at airport exchange counters before buying.
  3. Ask the booth staff to activate the data package and test internet access on your phone before walking away.
