Visa & Passport Guidelines

Travel documentation procedures for local citizens and international visitors.

Outbound Travel & Passport Regulations

1. Passport Issuance & Renewal

  • Official QR Code booking token required prior to office submission.
  • Original Citizenship Scrutiny Card (NRC) and Household List originals/copies required.
  • Apply via regional passport issuance authorities across major state capitals.

2. International Visa Exemption & Entry

  • Thailand: 14-day visa exemption applies when entering via air travel.
  • Singapore & Vietnam: Visa-free entry allowed for ASEAN passport holders up to specified durations.
  • Japan & South Korea: Requires prior e-Visa or sticker visa authorization through official embassy portals.

Entry Visa Protocols for Foreign Nationals

1. Tourist e-Visa

  • Apply strictly via the government portal (evisa.moip.gov.mm).
  • Standard processing timeline: 3 working days with a 28-day stay allowance.
  • Passport must maintain a minimum validity of 6 months from arrival date.

2. Business e-Visa

  • Requires an invitation letter from a company officially registered in Myanmar.
  • Valid company registration certificate (DICA) copy must be submitted.
  • Offers extended stay options subject to immigration approval.
